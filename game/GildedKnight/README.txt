REBINDING KEYS AND MUSIC
------------------------
Title screen > CONTROLS & MUSIC (or Esc during a fight > Controls & music).
Click a key box, then press the new key (or click the dashed box with a mouse
button to use Left / Middle / Right click). Every action has up to 3 boxes.
Esc cancels, Backspace clears a box. Esc, Enter, F, M, 1, 2 and 5 are reserved
for menus. Your choices are saved in the browser, so they stay next time.

All music is an original dark-fantasy jazz score generated inside the game with
upright bass, brushed percussion, reeds, muted brass, vibraphone and organ.
Every boss has a separate adaptive theme that intensifies during later phases,
along with matching title and victory pieces. Music and Effects have volume sliders.
The browser starts sound only after your first click or key press.

RELIC RARITIES AND DROP CHANCES
-------------------------------
Every rarity contains eight relic families: Attack, Defense, Skill Recovery,
Health, Stamina, Skill Attack Power, Stamina Recovery, and Flask Healing.
The rarity chance is rolled first; each of its eight relics is equally likely.

  COMMON (grey)      50% total / 6.25% per relic
    +5% attack/defense/skill recovery/skill power/stamina recovery/flask healing,
    +20 health, +10 stamina
  RARE (blue)        30% total / 3.75% per relic
    +15% attack/defense/skill recovery/skill power/stamina recovery/flask healing,
    +40 health, +30 stamina
  MYTHIC (purple)    15% total / 1.875% per relic
    +30% attack/defense/skill recovery/skill power/stamina recovery/flask healing,
    +80 health, +70 stamina
  LEGENDARY (yellow) 4% total / 0.5% per relic
    +50% attack/defense/skill recovery/skill power/stamina recovery/flask healing,
    +120 health, +100 stamina
  ANIS (red)         1% total / 0.125% per standard relic
    +100% attack/defense/skill recovery/skill power/stamina recovery/flask healing,
    +200 health, +150 stamina

  HARDY RELIC (black Anis) 0.01% exact independent chance
    Combines all eight Anis powers in one relic: +100% attack, defense, skill
    recovery, skill power, stamina recovery and flask healing, plus +200 health
    and +150 stamina.

Skill Attack Power applies only to active skills, not basic attacks. Stamina
Recovery speeds passive stamina regeneration. Flask Healing increases the amount
restored by each bottle. Common relics sell for 40 coins; higher rarities are kept.
The eight relic families now use distinct shard, shield, heart, hourglass, current,
sigil, coil, and chalice silhouettes while retaining their rarity colors.
